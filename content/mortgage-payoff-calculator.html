Paying Off Your Mortgage Early

Making extra payments on your mortgage can significantly reduce the total amount of interest you pay and shorten the life of your loan. Even a small additional amount each month can save you tens of thousands of dollars and help you own your home outright years sooner.

This calculator shows you the impact of applying an extra monthly payment to your mortgage's principal balance. It compares your original loan schedule to the new, accelerated schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Should I invest or pay off my mortgage early?

This is a common financial dilemma. Generally, if your mortgage interest rate is higher than the potential after-tax returns you could earn from investing, it makes more sense to pay down the mortgage. If your potential investment returns are higher, investing might be the better financial move. It also depends on your personal risk tolerance.

How do I make extra principal payments?

When making an extra payment, it's crucial to specify that the additional amount should be applied directly to the loan's "principal." If you don't, the lender might just count it as an early payment for the next month. Check with your lender on their specific process for making principal-only payments.
