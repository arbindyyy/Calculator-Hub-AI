Estimated Body Fat Percentage

The U.S. Navy Method

This calculator uses a method developed by the U.S. Navy to estimate body fat percentage based on simple circumference measurements. It provides a convenient way to track your body composition without the need for special equipment.

The formula uses your height, neck, and waist measurements. For women, it also includes the hip measurement to account for differences in body shape and fat distribution.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How accurate is this method?

The U.S. Navy method is considered a reasonably accurate estimation for most people. However, it is an estimate. For a precise measurement of body fat, you would need clinical methods like hydrostatic weighing or DEXA scans. This calculator is best used for tracking changes in your body composition over time.

Why is body fat percentage important?

Body fat percentage can be a better indicator of health than weight or BMI alone. It distinguishes between fat mass and lean mass (muscles, bones, organs). Having a healthy body fat percentage is associated with a lower risk of various chronic diseases.
