How to Use the Date Calculator

This tool helps you calculate a future or past date by adding or subtracting a specific duration. Simply enter a start date, then input the number of years, months, weeks, or days you wish to add or subtract. The calculator will instantly show you the resulting date.

This is useful for planning events, tracking deadlines, or calculating historical dates without having to manually count days on a calendar.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the calculator handle leap years?

The calculator's logic is based on JavaScript's built-in Date object, which automatically accounts for leap years when adding or subtracting days and years. You don't need to worry about the extra day in February during a leap year.

What if I add months and it results in a day that doesn't exist?

The Date object handles this gracefully. For example, if you add 1 month to January 31st, the result will be March 3rd (or March 2nd in a leap year), not February 31st. It correctly rolls over to the next month.
