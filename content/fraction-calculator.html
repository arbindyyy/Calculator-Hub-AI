How to Use the Fraction Calculator

This tool allows you to add, subtract, multiply, and divide two fractions. Simply enter the numerator and denominator for each fraction, select the desired operation, and the calculator will display the simplified result.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How are fractions simplified?

The calculator simplifies the resulting fraction by finding the Greatest Common Divisor (GCD) of the numerator and denominator. Both numbers are then divided by the GCD to produce the simplest form of the fraction.

What is an improper fraction?

An improper fraction is one where the numerator is larger than the denominator (e.g., 5/3). This calculator will display results as improper fractions if applicable, rather than mixed numbers (e.g., 1 2/3).
