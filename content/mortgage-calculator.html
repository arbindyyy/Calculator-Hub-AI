Understanding Your Mortgage

A mortgage is a loan used to purchase or maintain a home, land, or other types of real estate. The borrower agrees to pay the lender over time, typically in a series of regular payments that are divided into principal and interest. The property then serves as collateral to secure the loan.

How are payments calculated?

The formula for calculating your monthly mortgage payment is based on the loan principal, the interest rate, and the term of the loan. Our calculator uses this formula to provide an accurate estimate of your monthly payment, helping you budget effectively.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is included in a monthly mortgage payment?

Typically, a monthly mortgage payment includes principal, interest, property taxes, and homeowners' insurance (PITI). Our calculator estimates the principal and interest portion of your payment.

How can I lower my monthly payment?

You can lower your monthly payment by making a larger down payment, finding a lower interest rate, or choosing a longer loan term. However, a longer term may mean paying more in total interest over the life of the loan.
